* In the "mixed" protocol of Altai et al. the same subjects appear in training and test; by
  the authors' own account it gives a 59 % lower RMSE than leaving subjects out. The
  leave-subjects-out row is the comparable one.

  to none of them.
* The tasks do not match: both learned models are evaluated over tasks that include stairs,
  which are not processed here, and both are trained on subjects of this data set, which
  SoleID never saw. The rows are context, not a ranking.

## Not convertible: correlation

r, R^2 and CMC cannot be turned into an RMSE; they carry different information (shape rather
than magnitude) and are reported alongside. The Wang layer shows why: the vertical-only arm
has a higher hip correlation than SoleID with IMU kinematics, but a worse RMSE.
